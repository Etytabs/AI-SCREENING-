# ShakaHive — Company Foundation

**Company:** ShakaHive  
**Category:** Research Intelligence  
**First product:** AI-Assisted Grant Screening  
**Product working name:** HiveScreen (proposed; requires team approval)  
**Status:** Foundation proposal for team review  
**Origin:** AI for Public Good Incubation Program, University of Rwanda / College of Science and Technology.

## Identity

ShakaHive is a research intelligence company building AI-assisted tools that help institutions organise research information, screen grant applications, retrieve evidence and support transparent decisions.

**Positioning:** Research Intelligence.  
**Brand promise:** Intelligence that connects research to impact.  
**Core principle:** AI surfaces signals and evidence; authorised people remain accountable for consequential decisions.

## Mission

To build trusted AI-powered research intelligence that makes research funding and innovation processes more efficient, transparent and evidence-driven.

## Vision

To become a trusted research intelligence platform helping institutions make better-informed research funding, monitoring and innovation decisions.

## Values

- Evidence before assertion.
- Responsible AI and human accountability.
- Privacy, security and respect for data ownership.
- Transparency and traceability.
- Collaboration and institutional interoperability.
- Measurable public and research impact.

## Product architecture

ShakaHive is the company and platform identity. AI-Assisted Grant Screening is the first product. Grant lifecycle intelligence, monitoring and evaluation, and research/publication reconciliation are potential future modules, not commitments to deliver in the first MVP.

## Initial market context

The first use case is institutional grant screening in the Rwandan research ecosystem, including NCST/NRIF-aligned workflows. This is a validation context, not a claim of official NCST endorsement, production deployment, RIGMS access or partnership.

## Product principles

1. Start with a validated institutional workflow.
2. Combine deterministic rules with ML/NLP where semantic interpretation is needed.
3. Attach source provenance and evidence to findings.
4. Never equate similarity with plagiarism or novelty signals with scientific judgement.
5. Keep human review and recorded rationale in consequential decisions.
6. Clearly label synthetic/demo data and unavailable sources.
7. Do not claim integrations until authorised and tested.

## Team governance

The five roles and responsibilities are defined in the team's mutual agreement. This repository supplements that agreement; it does not replace it or establish equity, legal ownership, vesting or founder rights. Those require a separate signed agreement.

## Company operating system

- GitHub Issues: tasks, bugs, risks and decisions requiring action.
- GitHub Projects: cross-functional roadmap, status and milestones.
- Pull Requests: reviewable changes to code and documentation.
- `company/`: company, product, business, brand and operations records.
- `docs/`: technical specifications, architecture, AI/ML, security and pilot documentation.
- `resources/`: approved templates, meeting records, reports and working resources. Do not commit confidential applicant data, secrets or personal information.
- `assets/brand/`: approved logos and brand files.

## Document governance

Every maintained document should identify an owner, status (Draft / In Review / Approved / Archived), last updated date, approver where applicable, and links to related decisions or work items. Proposed policies become official only after team review and approval.

## Current truth and limitations

The public prototype uses synthetic/demo data. The repository's workflow documentation records limitations including demo identity headers, in-memory workflow state, RIGMS integration not implemented, optional external connectors not configured, and OCR for scanned PDFs not implemented. Production readiness must be assessed separately.

## Approval

This is a proposed company foundation. The team should review and approve the mission, vision, positioning, values, product naming, governance and brand direction before treating them as official.
